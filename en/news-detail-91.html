ALE | VT-Smart Lighting: Enhancing In-Cabin Experiences with Adaptive Lighting Technology

← Back to News
From March 26 to 28, 2025, the 20th Automotive Lighting Industry Development Forum and Shanghai International Automotive Lighting Exhibition (ALE) were held at the Kunshan Huaqiao International Expo Center. Voyager Group's VT-Smart Lighting series made its official debut, showcasing its innovative blend of advanced technology and aesthetic design to deliver efficient, intelligent in-cabin lighting solutions for the global automotive industry.
During the event, VT-Smart Lighting captured the attention of leading automakers and suppliers, including Audi, Changan, Li Auto, and Hyundai, as well as media representatives. As automotive intelligence accelerates, VT-Smart Lighting is set to drive the evolution of vehicle lighting toward smarter, more personalized, and multi-sensory experiences, empowering the future of intelligent cabin technology and creating quality mobility experiences for users worldwide.
