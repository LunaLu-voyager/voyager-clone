ESG

Environmental, Social and Governance

VOYISL places great importance on fulfilling its responsibilities across environmental, social and governance dimensions,
embedding ESG principles into management decisions and daily operations: the board of directors leads the company’s environmental, social and governance strategy and executes ESG policies and practices.

EnvironmentEnvironment

VOYISL strives to promote environmental protection, making progress in daily operations, office practices and production environmental responsibility — committed to environmentally friendly operations, advocating paperless offices, and certified to ISO14001 and ISO45001.

SocialSocial

Following the trend, serving as a bridge for deep economic and cultural integration across the Strait and supporting young people’s innovation, entrepreneurship and employment; devoted to public welfare, focusing on cultivating young people’s scientific thinking, and believing technology makes the world better.

GovernanceGovernance

Committed to maintaining high standards of corporate governance, advocating the philosophy of “participation by all, shared governance”, adhering to transparent management and leading the industry toward a more harmonious and efficient future.

Environment
Committed to Environmentally Friendly Operations
Advocating a Paperless Office
Certified to ISO14001 & ISO45001

We implement classified collection and regulated disposal of waste, entrust qualified third-party agencies for treatment, and maintain hazardous waste ledgers to ensure traceable management.

We continuously promote efficient resource utilization by integrating energy-saving requirements into daily operations management, advocating green and paperless offices, and implementing energy-saving measures such as turning off lights when leaving and keeping summer air-conditioning temperatures no lower than 26°C to effectively reduce resource consumption.

We actively respond to the national dual-carbon strategy by incorporating carbon management into our operations and control system, leveraging renewable energy applications to consolidate a low-carbon operations foundation and support green sustainable development.

The “Pearl Recycling Program” initiated by the Zhejiang Xinhua Love Education Foundation was held at VOYISL’s Pinghu manufacturing base. Sep 2025

In September 2025, Deputy Director-General Zong Wen of the Economic Affairs Bureau of the State Council Taiwan Affairs Office led a delegation to VOYISL for a research visit, gaining an in-depth understanding of VOYISL’s innovation achievements and cross-Strait industrial collaboration practices through on-site inspection, technology demonstrations and discussions.

Aug 20-25, 2025

The 2025 Cross-Strait Youth Forum was officially held in Shanghai. Chen Yinren, founder and CEO of VOYISL, attended as a specially invited entrepreneur and shared VOYISL’s entrepreneurial growth journey with more than 600 young people from across the Strait.

Apr 15, 2025

Organized by the Pudong Working Committee of the Shanghai Association of Taiwan-Invested Enterprises, more than 20 young Taiwanese students, mainly from Fudan University, visited VOYISL for an in-depth exchange themed on “Technology Exploration + Career Growth”. During the event, VOYISL launched the “Zhilian Hu-Tai · 2025 Taiwan Youth Internship Express Program” to build a fast track for young Taiwanese students’ career development on the mainland.

Jul 17, 2023

The 2023 “Putuo Yujing Program” — Cross-Strait Youth Enterprise Practice Training Camp officially opened. Leaders including Yang Lihua, Deputy Director of the Shanghai Taiwan Affairs Office, and Wang Jue, Deputy District Mayor of Putuo District, attended the ceremony, together with about 70 representatives from host and supporting organizations, internship enterprises, camp mentors and campers. As one of the strictly selected cross-Strait youth enterprise practice bases this year, VOYISL was invited to attend the opening ceremony.
