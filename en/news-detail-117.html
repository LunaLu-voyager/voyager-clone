Leading the 15th Five-Year Plan Period, Voyager Tech Honors the Commitment of the Era with Proprietary Technologies

As the country rolls out the planning for the 15th Five-Year Plan, the 26th National Science and Technology Week (May 24 – 31) was launched. Centered on the theme of Striving for New Scientific and Technological Achievements in the 15th Five-Year Plan Period, this event aims to translate cutting-edge technologies out of laboratories, deepen the integration of advanced technologies with diverse productive factors, and accelerate the commercialization and application of technical achievements in the real economy.


The 15th Five-Year Plan explicitly prioritizes low-altitude economy and intelligent driving as strategic emerging industries for intensive cultivation, and lists embodied intelligence for forward-looking layout among future industries. As a local Chinese tech enterprise, Voyager Tech actively responds to national calls for scientific and technological innovation, rides the industrial megatrend, and delivers solid outcomes through technological breakthroughs.


Core Highlights Aligned with the 15th Five-Year Plan
Keyword 1: Independent and Controllable Core Technologies

The 15th Five-Year Plan lays out a strategic arrangement to strengthen the independent controllability of core key technologies and enhance the security and resilience of industrial and supply chains. The state mandates accelerated breakthroughs in technical bottlenecks across intelligent perception, intelligent driving, low-altitude applications, artificial intelligence and other fields, so as to consolidate the foundation for independent innovation.



Since its establishment, Voyager Tech has steadfastly adhered to the full-stack independent R&D path. Aligning closely with national strategic priorities, we focus on forward-looking sectors including smart mobility, low-altitude economy and embodied intelligence. We independently develop technologies centered on intelligent perception as well as perception-and-decision integration, firmly securing full initiative in the R&D, iteration and commercial deployment of core technologies, and facilitating independent controllability of key links within the industrial chain.


Keyword 2: Building a Robust Security Barrier for Connected and Intelligent Vehicles

The outline of the 15th Five-Year Plan mandates the formulation and improvement of regulatory frameworks for intelligent connected vehicles, a full-lifecycle standard system covering the entire industrial chain, and accelerated development of pivotal core standards. Standardization will guide the safe and compliant rollout of autonomous driving technologies and underpin the bottom-line security of the industry.


Voyager Tech’s self-developed high-performance VT-CMS (Camera Monitoring System / Electronic Rearview Mirror System) reduces traditional blind spots and improves imaging clarity under harsh weather conditions to bolster driving safety, and has passed the national standard GB 15084-2022 certification. Our VT-DMS (Driver Monitoring System) continuously detects driver fatigue and distracted behavior and triggers timely warnings to upgrade overall travel safety for drivers and passengers.



Keyword 3: Boosting Orderly Development of Low-Altitude Economy & Exploring Full-Link Technical Routes for Embodied Intelligence

The 15th Five-Year Plan outline pushes for sound and orderly development of the low-altitude economy, accelerated R&D of new-type low-altitude equipment, breakthroughs in core technologies such as intelligent flight and low-altitude safety prevention and control, and strengthened safeguards for low-altitude flight operations.



Furthermore, the Plan designates embodied intelligence as a key future industry for cultivation, requiring the forward-looking development of an integrated full-chain technical system covering Perception – Decision – Execution, to seize the commanding heights of next-generation cutting-edge technologies.


Drawing on our accumulated vehicle-grade technologies in intelligent perception, decision-making and control, Voyager Tech speeds up cross-industry transplantation and reuse of mature solutions. Our technologies are being extended and deployed across diversified scenarios such as drones and humanoid robots, highly matching the two national strategic priorities of low-altitude economy and embodied intelligence.


Industrial Value of Cross-Domain Technology Reuse
  • Lower Development Costs
    Direct porting of core technologies eliminates the need to build underlying architectures from scratch, drastically cutting early-stage R&D expenses for new business lines.
  • Shorter R&D Cycles
    Modular migration and rapid iteration of proven technologies effectively shorten product development and validation lead times.
  • Superior Cross-Scenario Reliability
    Having undergone stringent vehicle-grade validation, our solutions deliver stronger adaptability and reliability in low-altitude logistics delivery, aerial inspection and other scenarios.


    Pragmatic Implementation: A Practitioner of National Strategy Rollout

  1. Uphold Full-Stack Independent R&D to Secure Core Technology Sovereignty
    We sharpen competitive edges in visual environmental perception, path planning and tracking, deviation correction and mapping to guarantee fully independent and controllable core technologies.
  2. Scale Up Digital & Intelligent R&D with Heavy Investment in Algorithms and AI
    We keep optimizing the multi-modal fusion algorithm framework and scale up investment in artificial intelligence to boost computing and decision-making capabilities in complex scenarios. Powered by self-developed AI cameras equipped with AI-ISP chips, our ISP algorithms together with AI model-driven 3D noise reduction technology achieve breakthrough high-definition imaging under low-light conditions such as underground parking garages and mountainous night roads.
  3. Deepen Industry-University-Research Collaboration to Accelerate Industrial Translation
    We partner with universities to tackle frontier technological challenges, shorten the journey from laboratory research to mass production, and tightly connect advanced technologies with the real economy.
  4. Drive Industrial Upgrading via Technological Innovation
    Leveraging our strengths in technology reuse and innovation capacity, we steadily advance the productization and mass production of solutions for emerging sectors, injecting vitality into the real economy.
Riding the development momentum of the 15th Five-Year Plan, Voyager Tech stays grounded in pragmatic original aspirations. We remain a down-to-earth executor of national strategies and a long-term contributor to the high-quality development of China’s intelligent mobility industry.
