Voyager Tech Receives Scientific Research Awards from the Ministry of Education

Recently, Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China announced the winners of the Scientific Research Awards (Natural Sciences and Engineering Technology). Voyager Tech was honored with the Second Prize of Engineering Technology Research Achievement Award for its project titled Key Technologies and Applications of Vision-Fused Mobile Robot Navigation Control in Complex Environments. This award represents notable recognition of technological achievements in China's higher education and research sectors, with rigorous evaluation criteria and strong research value.



The award-winning project addresses key challenges in vision-fused mobile robot navigation control under complex environments, resolving industry difficulties related to high-precision operation in complex dynamic scenarios. It provides strong support for core product lines including the VT-Pilot®, VT-Cockpit® and VT-Camera Series, and helps advance Voyager Tech's three strategic segments: ground mobility, low-altitude economy and operational automation.


This achievement further demonstrates Voyager Tech's capabilities in cross-domain technology application. Vision-based perception, planning and control technologies transcend the limits of single scenarios, extending from intelligent vehicles to smart mobility, low-altitude economy, industrial automation and other fields, forming a reusable and scalable intelligent ecosystem for full-scenario applications.


Mr. Brian Chen, Founder and CEO of Voyager Tech, stated: "Technological innovation underpins the development of Voyager Tech. Our R&D team remains committed to scientific research and focuses on developing technologies aligned with national strategies and socioeconomic needs. Industry-university-research cooperation enhances our innovation and promotes the development of practical, high-value solutions. Receiving this award from the Ministry of Education is an acknowledgment of our past efforts and encouragement for our future development. As the company leader, I am confident that Voyager Tech will continue to fulfill its corporate mission and responsibilities, deliver more innovative technologies to the industry, drive industrial progress and empower people's smart mobility life.u201D
