Voyager Tech Attends the 2025 Cross-Strait Youth Forum to Promote Youth Exchange

From August 20 to 25, the 2025 Cross-Strait Youth Forum was officially held in Shanghai. The event was co-hosted by the Shanghai Association for Cross-Strait Exchanges and Promotion, the Ma Ying-jeou Foundation, the Shanghai Association of Taiwan Investment Enterprises and the Shanghai Exchange Center for Taiwanese Compatriots and Their Families, building a cultural exchange platform for young people from both sides of the strait.


Zhao Shitong, Deputy Director of the Taiwan Affairs Office of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council, Wu Wei, Member of the Standing Committee of the CPC Shanghai Municipal Committee and Executive Vice Mayor of Shanghai, Hsiao Hsu-tsen, Executive Director of the Ma Ying-jeou Foundation, and Liu Ping, Deputy Secretary-General of the Shanghai Municipal People's Government attended the event and delivered speeches. Brian Chen, Founder and CEO of Voyager Tech, attended the event as a specially invited entrepreneur and shared Voyager Tech's entrepreneurial and growth journey with more than 600 young people from both sides of the strait.


Voyager Tech has always been committed to promoting the integrated development of cross-strait economy and culture, and actively supports young people from both sides in pursuing, building and realizing their dreams in the field of science and technology. In his speech, Chen said: “As an entrepreneur, it is a great honor to participate in such an event. We hope to provide more development opportunities for young people through our corporate platform, so that young people from both sides of the strait can enhance mutual understanding through culture and create the future together through innovation and entrepreneurship.”
