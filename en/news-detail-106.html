Voyager Tech Honored with 2026 Global Automotive Supply Chain Technological Innovation Ecosystem Partner Award

On March 13, 2026, the 2026 Automotive Supply Chain Ecosystem Partner Conference officially kicked off at the China International Exhibition and Convention Center. The event was hosted by China Automotive News—a definitive authority in the sector since 1984 and an affiliate of the People's Daily—under the guidance of the China Energy and Automobile Media Group. At the concurrent Global Automotive Supply Chain Ecosystem Partner Gala, Voyager Tech was honored with the Technological Innovation Ecosystem Partner Award. This accolade recognizes the company’s robust full-stack self-development technology system and its "hardcore" technical expertise across VT-Camera smart vision sensing, VT-Pilot® smart assisted driving system, and VT-Cockpit® smart cockpit series.




This award stands as a powerful testament to Voyager Tech's independent technical prowess. Leveraging its core product portfolios—VT-Pilot®, VT-Cockpit®, and VT-Camera—the company has successfully integrated its full-stack technologies into real-world automotive applications, serving both the passenger and commercial vehicle sectors with market-aligned solutions. Furthermore, the recognition underscores Voyager Tech's exceptional potential in technology versatility and scalability. Beyond fortifying its competitive edge in the automotive chain, the company is strategically expanding into emerging sectors such as low-altitude economy and embodied AI, empowering the global "Intelligent Manufacturing" industry and fostering a more innovative, thriving ecosystem.
